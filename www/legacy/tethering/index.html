A bare modem will not attach to the carrier until it is given an APN. Phones and HiLink dongles dial themselves and never need any of this — which is why this section only appears when something that needs it is plugged in.

Setup Guides
iPhone — USB tethering
  1. Plug the iPhone into the router's USB-A port with a Lightning/USB-C data cable.
  2. Unlock the phone and tap Trust on the "Trust This Computer?" prompt. It only appears once per router.
  3. Turn on Settings → Personal Hotspot → Allow Others to Join.
The ipheth driver plus usbmuxd handle the pairing. iOS hands out 172.20.10.0/28, which cannot collide with anything here. If nothing appears, unplug it, plug it back in, and tap Rescan — iOS sometimes refuses the handshake if the phone was locked at plug-in.
Android — USB tethering
  1. Plug the phone in, then set the USB mode to anything other than "charge only" if prompted.
  2. Enable Settings → Network & internet → Hotspot & tethering → USB tethering. The toggle only becomes tappable once the cable is in.
Android shows up as rndis_host (most phones) or cdc_ncm (newer Samsung/Pixel). Both drivers are installed. The usual subnet is 192.168.42.0/24 — no conflict.
Huawei E5577 and other MiFi units
Plug it in and it should just work — the E5577 presents itself as a network device and serves 192.168.9.0/24, which does not usually overlap the router's own networks or the upstream one. Its own admin page stays reachable at 192.168.9.1 while tethered.
Huawei HiLink dongles — read before plugging in
E3372h, E8372 and most HiLink sticks default to 192.168.8.1/24 — the exact address of this router's LAN. That is a genuine conflict, not a warning to click past: two identical /24 routes end up in the table and LAN traffic starts leaving through the USB port at random.
  1. Plug the dongle into a PC first and open its web UI.
  2. Change its LAN/DHCP range to something unused — 192.168.9.0/24 or 192.168.77.0/24.
  3. Then move it to the router.
If you plug one in unchanged, the router detects the overlap, refuses the uplink and tells you here rather than silently breaking your LAN.
5G / LTE modem sticks
Drivers for every common mode are installed: qmi_wwan, cdc_mbim, cdc_ncm, huawei_cdc_ncm, rndis_host, sierra_net and option for AT-command sticks, alongside usb-modeswitch with the full mode database.
  1. Plug in and wait about ten seconds.
  2. If the USB panel says mass storage — mode switch pending, tap Re-detect; that runs the mode-switch database against it.
  3. A stick that lands as a serial port instead of a network device shows up under Serial ports below. Those need an APN and a dial script, which this page does not do yet — tell me the model and I'll wire up the right protocol.
